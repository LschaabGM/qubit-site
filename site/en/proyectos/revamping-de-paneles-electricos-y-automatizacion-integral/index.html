Challenge

Our client needed to modernize its machine room and optimize the operation of its cold rooms and freezing tunnels. The challenge was to implement a solution that ensured maximum system availability and improved operational efficiency.

Solution

To meet this challenge, we carried out a full revamp of the electrical panels to upgrade and modernize their infrastructure. We implemented a full automation system with two redundant PLCs to ensure continuous system availability. We developed HMI and SCADA interfaces that let Americold view real-time data and historical records, which made it easier to supervise and control its operations. We configured a Modbus network to integrate data from the NH3 compressors, and we added an industrial IoT device for remote access and continuous 24/7 monitoring of the system.
