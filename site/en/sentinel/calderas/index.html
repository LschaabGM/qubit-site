Industry data

What not measuring costs you

Efficiency benchmarks for industrial boilers.

  • 2%extra fuel for each millimeter of common scale. Up to 7% with iron and silica deposits.
  • 8–10%of the feedwater is lost through blowdown.
  • 10%of the time, manual blowdown hits the correct conductivity range. The rest of the time, the boiler operates outside the setpoint and loses energy.
  • 1%efficiency gain for every 22 °C drop in flue gas temperature.

Source: U.S. Department of Energy. Standard industry values.

From corrective to predictive maintenance

Data defines the intervention, not the calendar or a failure that already occurred.

  • query_stats

    From calendar to actual condition

    Maintenance is no longer set by a fixed date. Data defines it: you intervene at the exact moment the equipment condition requires it.

  • water_drop

    Control of water quality and blowdown extends the interval between mechanical tube cleanings. It also reduces how often the plant shuts down and the related labor hours.

    A stuck blowdown valve shows up instantly in the trend, before it causes low water level, overheating, or a cracked tube.

How it works

SENTinel: the solution for your plant

  1. S

    Sensing

    Sensors that measure what you need: temperature, pressure, level, and water conductivity.

  2. E

    Edge

    A controller in the boiler room processes the data instantly and actuates the blowdown.

  3. N

    Cloud

    The platform brings all the information together and shows it on a single screen.

  4. T

    Decision-making

    AI analyzes the history, anticipates failures, and suggests when to intervene.
